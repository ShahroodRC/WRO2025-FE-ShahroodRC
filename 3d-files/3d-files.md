## 3D Files
This folder contains the 3D design files and rendered images for the robot.

- **Complete model:** `3d-files/robot_complete.io` — the complete robot model in the `.io` export format.
- **Pixy camera mount:** `3d-files/pixy-cam-mount.stl` — a 3D-printable STL mount designed to hold the Pixy 2.1 camera in the correct position and orientation for obstacle detection. The mount secures the Pixy to the robot chassis, provides a cable routing channel, and includes mounting holes compatible with small machine screws. Recommended print settings: PLA, 0.2 mm layer height, 15–25% infill; orient the mount flat on the print bed to preserve mounting-hole accuracy.
- **Renders & previews:** high-resolution images and screenshots generated from the 3D model for documentation and presentation purposes.

Use these files to inspect the mechanical layout, check component fits, or generate further renders and assembly guides.
