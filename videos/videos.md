## Videos
This folder contains recorded runs of the robot from both the Open and Obstacle challenges. The videos document performance, strategy runs, and final demonstrations.

- **Local files:** competition recordings and raw footage used for analysis (check this folder for `.mp4` files).
- **YouTube:** public uploads of our challenge runs:
	- Obstacle Challenge: https://youtu.be/onJv0w_JzZM
	- Open Challenge: https://youtu.be/PhpbAQ0mky4

Use these videos for review, sharing with judges, or uploading to team pages.
